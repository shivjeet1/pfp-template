## Privacy Policy

This portfolio website respects your digital privacy:

- **No Behavioral Tracking:** No tracking cookies, marketing beacons, or external ad networks are loaded.
- **Local Storage:** Light/dark theme preference is kept exclusively on your local device via standard browser `localStorage`.
- **Open Standards:** Built with open web technologies and served securely over HTTPS.
