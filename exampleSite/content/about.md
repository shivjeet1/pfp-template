## Background

I am a noble scion of the Kagenou clan, holder of the Mantera insignia, and the only member of my family still aware that any of this is happening. I wield the Shadow Affliction — a gift that arrived as a curse, deepened when I tried to exploit it, and has since eaten most of my remaining lifespan.

My life plan was immortality, then world domination, then a long and comfortable retirement. The curse took the first, mocked the second, and left me with a countdown I refuse to look at directly.
