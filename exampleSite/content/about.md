## Shadow

I lurk in the shadows and hunt the shadows.

Second son of a house that breeds swords. Lord of an organization that does not officially exist. Keeper of a magic that was never meant to work.

I do not explain myself. I am explained later — by others, in terms I did not choose.

---

## The Garden

Founded at ten. Named for a shadow. Grown into something that moves nations and files its own taxes.

It fights a cult that is entirely real. I remain the only member who believes it is a story we agreed to tell.

Seven answered questions I never asked. They follow. I permit it.

---

## The Wisdom

I mention things that do not exist here. Light that writes itself. Machines that remember faces. Music for a composer long dead.

They build them. They believe it is strategy.

---

## The Count

Two hundred years. Then three hundred — so that one of them would not leave first.

Six hundred is the estimate.

It is not enough.

It will be enough.
